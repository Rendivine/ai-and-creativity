Participant Information & Consent

Please read the following information before continuing.

About this research

This demonstration introduces a proposed academic research experience exploring the relationship between Artificial Intelligence and human creativity.

Your participation

You will be invited to complete creative activities designed to explore different aspects of creative thinking.

Privacy and confidentiality

The proposed research website will use participant codes rather than names wherever possible. Research information will be handled confidentially.

Voluntary participation

Participation is voluntary. You may choose not to participate. The final approved study will explain withdrawal procedures.

This is a demonstration consent page, not an approved consent form for actual research recruitment.
